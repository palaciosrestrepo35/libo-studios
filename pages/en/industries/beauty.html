Questions from salons and barbershops

Can clients book with a specific person?

Yes. The client picks the service and the person they want, and only sees that person's open time slots.

Does it connect with my Instagram?

Yes. We can show your posts in the website gallery, and your booking link goes in your bio.

Can I charge a deposit at booking?

Yes. With online payments the client leaves a deposit when they book, which greatly reduces appointments where nobody shows up.

Does it work if I have several locations?

Yes. Each location has its own team, its own hours and its own spot on Google Maps, and the client chooses where they want their appointment.

Can I sell products on the website?

Yes. You can sell the products you use in the salon and gift cards, with online payment or orders by WhatsApp.
