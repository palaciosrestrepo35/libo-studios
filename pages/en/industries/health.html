Before booking, patients do their research

Nobody picks a dentist or a psychologist at random. They search Google for "dentist near me" or "therapist in my area", compare profiles on Google Maps, read reviews and visit the website to see who will treat them, where they studied, which treatments they offer and how to get there.

If they cannot find that information, or if booking means calling during office hours and waiting on hold, they often keep looking.

Your website is the first consultation. It has to feel professional, answer questions and make booking easy.

Questions from clinics and practices

Can patients book on their own?

Yes. They see the open slots, pick theirs and get a confirmation and a reminder. You decide which hours to open and how long each type of appointment lasts.

What about my patients' information?

The site is secure, we ask only for what the appointment needs and we publish your privacy policy. Medical records are not handled on the website.

Does it work with several locations or several professionals?

Yes. Each location and each professional can have their own page, schedule and spot on Google Maps.

Can I publish treatment prices?

You can publish prices, price ranges, or just describe the treatments and ask patients to get in touch. We decide together based on how you work.

How do I get more Google reviews?

We give you a direct link so patients can leave a review after their visit, and we include it in the confirmation message.

How much does a website for a clinic or dentist cost?

Websites start from 400 USD. The final price depends on what your practice needs, such as online booking, several professionals or several locations, and you get it in a written quote after we listen to you.
