When the usual tools are not enough

There comes a point when the business grows and spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and generic software start getting in the way. The same information gets copied into three places, mistakes multiply and nobody has the full picture.

That is where software built to fit changes things: it does exactly what your operation needs, with your steps, your rules and your names. No paying for features you never use, and no bending the way you work around a program that was not made for you.

We build it in stages: we start with what hurts most, get it working quickly and grow from there.

Custom development FAQ

Do you work with large companies?

Yes. We build for small businesses and for large companies. On large projects we work in stages, with deliveries your team can test, and we coordinate with your technology department if you have one.

Can I start small and grow later?

Yes, and that is what we recommend. We start with whatever has the most impact, get it working and add features as you need them.

Is the software mine?

Yes. Everything we build for your company, including the data, is yours.

Can it connect to the programs I already use?

In most cases, yes. Payment providers, invoicing, Google Sheets, inventory and many other programs can be connected. We check this at the start of the project.

Can you move my WordPress or Wix site?

Yes. We move your pages, text and images to a new, faster site, and make the old addresses lead to the new ones so you keep your visits from Google.

You do not need to know anything about technology. Explain the problem and we will propose the solution.
