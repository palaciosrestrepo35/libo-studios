Online store FAQ

Which payment methods can I accept?

It depends on the payment provider you choose. With Stripe you can take international credit and debit cards. In Colombia, Wompi, Mercado Pago or PayU also let you accept PSE and other methods like Nequi. We help you pick the one that fits your business best.

Do I have to pay a monthly platform fee?

No. The store is built with our own code, so you do not pay a store platform every month. The payment provider does charge a fee on each sale, as with any store.

Is Shopify better than my own store?

Shopify is good for getting started quickly, but it charges a monthly fee in dollars and does not work with every Colombian payment provider. Your own store costs more up front, but has no monthly platform fee and adapts completely to your business.

How do I handle shipping?

We set up shipping costs by city or zone, free shipping above a certain amount, or in store pickup. You ship with the carrier you already use.

Can I change prices and products myself?

Yes. You get a dashboard to see orders, add products, change prices and mark items as sold out.

I already have a store on another platform. Can you move it?

Yes. We move your products, photos and text to your new store and make sure old links lead to the new ones, so you keep what you have earned on Google.
