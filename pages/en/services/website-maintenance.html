A neglected website shows

Old prices, expired promotions, hours that are no longer right, photos from years ago or a contact form that quietly stopped sending messages without anyone noticing. Website maintenance FAQ

What exactly does maintenance include?

Content changes, backups, security, downtime monitoring, domain, email and a monthly review on Google. The exact scope, such as how many changes per month, goes in writing in the proposal.

What happens if my website goes down?

Our monitoring alerts us, so we usually find out before you do. We look into it and get it running again as fast as possible, restoring a backup if needed.

Can you maintain a website someone else built?

Yes. First we review it to understand how it was built and what condition it is in, and we tell you honestly whether it is worth maintaining or rebuilding.

How do I request a change?

By WhatsApp or email, whichever is easier for you. Tell us what to change and we let you know when it is done.

Do the domain and the website still belong to me?

Yes. The domain is in your business's name and the website is yours. If you ever decide not to continue, we hand everything over to you.
