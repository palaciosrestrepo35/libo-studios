Process

From your idea to a website that sells

Four clear steps, with dates in writing and progress you can see while it is being built. At every moment you know what is being done, what comes next and when your website goes live.

01

Conversation

We understand your business before we talk about websites.

You tell us what you sell, who your customers are, how they find you today and what you want to achieve: more orders, more appointments, more calls or selling in other cities and countries. It can happen over WhatsApp, a phone call or a video call, wherever you are.

What you get

  • An honest recommendation of what you need and what you do not
  • Ideas from what other businesses like yours do well
02

Proposal

Everything in writing, before we start.

We send you a proposal with the price, what it includes, what it does not include and the delivery date. Websites start from 400 USD, and your final price comes in this written quote, once we have listened to what you need. No fine print and no charges that show up later. If something does not convince you, we adjust it.

What you get

  • A fixed price
  • A detailed scope
  • A delivery date
03

Design and development

You watch it grow instead of waiting in the dark.

We design and code your website, phone first. We share a link with the progress so you can try it and leave comments. We adjust until it is exactly what you need.

What you get

  • A private link to follow the progress
  • Adjustments within the agreed scope
  • Text and photos organized for Google
04

Launch and support

Live, on Google and in your hands.

We publish the website on your domain, submit it to Google, check that everything works on phones and computers, and show you how to use it. After launch, you keep having support.

What you get

  • Your website live on your domain
  • Your website submitted to Google
  • A guide to using it
  • Support after launch

Questions about the process

How long until my website is ready?

It depends on what you need. A simple website takes much less time than a store with payments or custom software. The exact date goes in writing in the proposal, before we start.

Can I ask for changes while it is being built?

Yes. We share a link with the progress so you can review it and leave comments. How adjustments are handled is set out in writing in the proposal.

What do I need to have ready to start?

Knowing what you sell and who you sell to is enough for the first conversation. After that we ask for your logo, photos, prices and text. If you do not have them, we help you put them together.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your business's name and the website is yours.

What happens after launch?

We show you how to use it and you keep having support. If you want us to handle changes, security and your follow-up on Google, you can add our website maintenance service.

A website does not end the day it goes live

That is when it starts working for your business. The first step is a conversation.
