For florists, gift shops, gift baskets, surprise breakfast deliveries and boutiques. A beautiful catalog where your customers choose a gift, personalize it and send you the order by WhatsApp, and a website that shows up on Google when someone looks for a gift in your city.

Gifts are bought in a hurry

People shopping for a gift almost always need it today or tomorrow. They search Google for "gift delivery" or "flowers near me", look at two or three options on their phone and message the one that answers first and shows clear prices.

If your shop only lives on Instagram, that customer will not find you. And if they do, they have to ask for prices one by one. A website with a catalog solves both problems: you show up on Google, and the customer sees everything, chooses and writes to you with the order ready.

On dates like Valentine's Day, Mother's Day or Christmas, that is the difference between handling ten orders or fifty.

What your shop needs

  • A catalog by category and by occasion
  • Personalization options: colors, message, name, size
  • A cart that sends the order by WhatsApp, fully written out
  • Online payments if you want to get paid without stepping in
  • Delivery fees by neighborhood or town
  • Free product listings on Google Shopping
  • A page for each city or town you deliver to
  • Gift guides by occasion that bring visitors from Google

Ready for special dates

Special dates are your high season, and your competitors' too. We get your website ready ahead of time: a section for each date, featured products, delivery messages and last day to order notices. That way you reach the date already showing up on Google, not scrambling to get there.

We are based in Medellín, Colombia, and work with gift shops and florists anywhere in the world by video call and WhatsApp.

Catalog or online store?

If you prefer to confirm each order and get paid by bank transfer, or by Nequi in Colombia, the catalog with WhatsApp ordering is the fastest option. If you sell nationwide and want to take card payments without stepping in, with PSE in Colombia, an online store is the better fit. We help you choose.

Questions from gift shops and florists

Can my customers personalize the gift on the website?

Yes. They can choose colors, size and wrapping, and write the message or name they want on the gift. All of that comes with the order.

Can I charge different delivery fees by neighborhood?

Yes. We set the delivery fee by neighborhood, zone or town, and the customer sees it before sending the order.

How do I show up on Google for Valentine's Day or Mother's Day?

By preparing the website weeks ahead: a section or guide for each date with the right products. Google needs time to show it, so it does not pay to wait until the last week.

Can my products appear on Google Shopping?

Yes. If each product has its own page with a name, photo and price, we can list it on Google Shopping for free. Detalles de Amor has 58 products there.

What if I change products every season?

You can easily add, remove or mark products as sold out, or ask us to make the changes through website maintenance.

How much does a website for a gift shop cost?

Websites start from 400 USD. The final price depends on what your shop needs, such as the size of the catalog, online payments or delivery zones, and you get it in a written quote after we listen to you.
