Detalles de Amor: gifts that sell themselves

Detalles de Amor makes personalized gifts in Medellín, Colombia: preserved roses, surprise breakfasts, resin keepsakes, baked goods and gift boxes. We built their website so customers find them on Google, choose their gift and send the order on WhatsApp, at any hour.

The challenge

Detalles de Amor sold mostly through Instagram and WhatsApp. Every sale started the same way: the customer asked for prices, photos had to be sent one by one, then came the personalization options and delivery costs, and the order was put together by hand. A lot of time per sale, and customers who left when the reply took too long.

On top of that, people searching Google for "gift delivery in Medellín" or "preserved roses in Medellín" could not find the shop. Other stores were winning those searches.

What we built

  • A catalog by category: preserved roses, surprise breakfasts, chocolate covered strawberries, gift baskets, gift boxes, resin, baked goods, keepsakes and occasions
  • A blog with 47 gift idea articles, written for what people search on Google
  • A page for every product, with photos, price range and description
  • A cart that puts the order together and sends it on WhatsApp with one click
  • The full site in Spanish and English
  • Pages for delivery in Medellín, Envigado, Sabaneta, Itagüí and Bello
  • A gift guide by occasion: birthdays, anniversaries, Colombia's Love and Friendship Day, Mother's Day and more
  • An assistant that knows the whole catalog and answers in both languages

How it looks

How we made it visible on Google

One page for every search

Instead of one page for everything, every product, category, town and occasion has its own page. That way the shop can show up when someone searches for "surprise breakfast in Medellín" or "gift delivery in Envigado".

Google understands its products and prices

We organized each product's information so Google knows what it is, how much it costs and whether it is available. That is why 58 products show up for free on Google Shopping.

Google Maps business profile

Its Google Maps profile is connected to the website, and we track where it ranks on the map area by area to know where to improve.

Daily check

Every day we check which pages Google is showing, so we can catch and fix any page that gets left out.
