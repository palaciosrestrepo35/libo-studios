La Tienda de la Manzana: iPhones and phones people find on Google

La Tienda de la Manzana sells new and used iPhones, Samsung, Xiaomi and POCO phones, accessories and the full Apple lineup from its shop in the Palace Junín arcade in downtown Medellín, Colombia. We built their website so anyone looking for a phone in Medellín finds them on Google, compares models, colors and prices, and sends the order on WhatsApp with one click, at any hour.

The challenge

Medellín has hundreds of phone stores, and customers almost always start on Google with searches like "iPhone 17 Pro price Colombia" or "used iPhone Medellín". Without a website of its own, other stores were winning those searches.

On top of that, every WhatsApp sale started from scratch. The customer asked for model, storage, color and price, and photos were sent one by one. Anyone who wanted to trade in a used phone had to describe its condition over several messages.

What we built

  • A catalog by category: new and used phones, the Apple lineup, accessories, audio, chargers, gaming, Meta glasses and gear for content creators
  • A page for every model, with its table of versions and starting price
  • Color and storage selectors that update the price, the photo and the WhatsApp message instantly
  • A cart that puts the full order together and sends it on WhatsApp with one click
  • A "Sell your phone" quote tool where customers upload photos of their used phone. The photos reach the store through private links and delete themselves after 7 days
  • 29 buying guides written for the searches people actually make on Google
  • The whole store in English too, for tourists and foreigners shopping in Medellín
  • An installment calculator from 6 to 36 months on every product, plus paying a deposit and the balance on delivery
  • A chat assistant that answers questions and takes customers to WhatsApp with the message already written
  • Real Google reviews and the store's map
  • Light and dark mode, plus tracking of every WhatsApp click with Google Analytics
  • Each product's information organized for Google, and automatic alerts to Bing on every change

How we make it visible on Google

One page for every search

Every model, category and guide has its own page. That way the store can show up when someone searches for "iPhone 17 Pro Max price Colombia" or "used iPhone Medellín".

Guides that answer what people ask

The 29 buying guides compare models, explain prices in Colombia and clear up questions like eSIM versus physical SIM. They bring in visitors who are still deciding what to buy.

Google understands its products and prices

We organized each product's information so Google knows what it is, how much it costs and whether it is available in the store.

Search engines notified right away

Every time the website changes, we notify Google and Bing so they see the new prices and products without waiting weeks.
