An assistant that answers for you, any time of day

Your customers ask questions at 11 at night

Do you deliver to my area? How much is the large arrangement? Are you open on Sunday? Many of the questions you get are the same ones, over and over, and they arrive when you cannot answer. Every unanswered question is a customer who goes looking somewhere else.

The assistant answers those questions in seconds, using the real information about your business. It recommends products, explains how to order, shares links and, when the conversation calls for it, hands the customer over to your WhatsApp so you can close the sale.

It does not replace your team. It takes the repetitive work off their plate so they can focus on what truly needs a person.

AI chatbot FAQ

What happens if the assistant does not know the answer?

It says so honestly and offers the customer the option to message you on WhatsApp. We set it up to answer only with your business information and never make up facts.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Spanish and most other languages. It replies in whatever language the customer writes in.

Can it work as a WhatsApp AI assistant?

Yes. From your website, the assistant can hand the customer over to your WhatsApp along with the context of the conversation. We can also put an assistant on your business's own WhatsApp.

Does it replace the people who serve customers?

No. It handles the repeated questions and covers after hours, so your team can spend its time closing sales and on the cases that need a person.

How is it kept up to date?

When your prices, products or hours change, its information gets updated. If you have website maintenance, we take care of it for you.
