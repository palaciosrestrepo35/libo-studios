No more "how much is this?" a hundred times a day

If you sell on WhatsApp or Instagram, you know the routine: sending photos one by one, answering prices, asking for size, color and address, and writing up the order by hand. It wastes time, and you lose sales when you cannot reply fast enough.

With an online catalog, the customer sees everything you sell, with prices and availability. They choose, add to the cart and tap a button. You get a WhatsApp message with the complete order: products, quantities, total and their details.

You keep selling on WhatsApp, the way your customers like it, without the work of putting each order together.

WhatsApp catalog FAQ

How is this different from the WhatsApp Business catalog?

The WhatsApp Business catalog is only visible to people who already have you saved. An online catalog has its own address, shows up on Google, can be shared on any social network and delivers the order already built with the total.

Do I need an online payment provider?

No. You can get paid the way you already do: bank transfer, cash on delivery, or Nequi and Daviplata in Colombia. If you want to take payments online later, you can move up to an online store.

How do I update prices and products?

We give you a simple way to change prices, add products and mark items as sold out. If you would rather not do it yourself, website maintenance covers it.

Can orders go to more than one number?

Yes. Orders can go to one main number or be split up, for example by location or by product type.

Does it work if I sell a lot of products?

Yes. With categories, search and filters, the catalog works just as well with 20 products as with hundreds.
