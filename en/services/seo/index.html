What SEO is, in plain words

SEO is everything you do so Google shows your website near the top when someone searches for something like "bakery near me" or "employment lawyer in Medellín". Those first results get almost all the clicks. Hardly anyone sees the ones on page two.

Google decides the order based on how well your website answers what the person is looking for, how fast and easy it is to use, how close your business is, and how much trust it earns: reviews, mentions and other sites that recommend you.

Our job is to improve each of those things, one by one, and measure whether it is working.

What we do

  • We find the searches your real customers make
  • We adjust titles, text and pages for those searches

Local SEO and beyond: where people will find you

On Google

In the regular results, when someone searches for your service or product. It is traffic you get without paying for ads, and it keeps coming month after month.

On Google Maps

On the map and in the three businesses Google shows at the top for local searches, like "barbershop near me". We work on your business profile, photos, hours and reviews, and we track your Google Maps ranking area by area.

On Google Shopping

If you sell products, they can appear for free in the Google shopping tab with a photo and price. We take care of listing them and keeping them approved.

On ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity

More and more people ask an artificial intelligence assistant before they search. We organize your information so those assistants know about you and recommend you too.

Real results, not promises

For Detalles de Amor, a gift shop in Medellín, the site now has 152 pages that appear on Google, 58 products on Google Shopping and a score of 100/100 on the Ubersuggest site check for Google. We track its Google Maps position by area and review what Google shows every day.

SEO FAQ

How long until I see results?

The first changes on Google Maps and in searches with little competition can show up within a few weeks. Highly competitive searches take months. That is why we measure and report progress every month.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It is your business listing that appears on Google Maps and next to the search results: name, address, hours, photos, phone number and reviews. It is free and it is the most important piece for local SEO, showing up in local searches.

How do I improve my Google Maps ranking?

With a complete, verified business profile, the right category, real photos, up to date hours, customer reviews and a website that confirms the same information. We take care of all of it.

Is this the same as paying for Google ads?

No. Ads appear while you pay and disappear when you stop. SEO brings visits without paying for each click, and the results last over time.

Can you improve a website you did not build?

Yes. We review your current website and tell you what can be improved. If it has deeper problems, like being very slow, building a new one is sometimes the better option.

Does it help me show up on ChatGPT?

It helps. Artificial intelligence assistants take information from websites that are clear, well organized and mentioned by other sites. What works for Google works for them too.

How does Google see you today?

Send us your website or your business name and we will tell you what is holding your ranking back.
