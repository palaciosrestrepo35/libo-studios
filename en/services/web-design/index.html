Website design FAQ

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size. A single page website is ready much sooner than a site with several pages, a blog or multiple languages. Before we start, you get the delivery date in writing.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from 400 USD, which makes this affordable web design for a small business that wants a site of its own. The final price depends on what you need: the number of pages, whether we write the text or take photos, and extras like bookings or online payments. We listen to what you need first, then send you a written quote with the final price, with no surprise charges later.

Can I build a free website for my business?

Yes. With tools like Wix, Google Sites or your Google Business Profile you can make a simple website without paying. They are fine to get started, but they usually show the platform's ads, use an address that is not yours and load slowly, and that costs your business trust and spots on Google. When you want a website of your own, on your own domain and built to bring in customers, we can help you build it.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?

You do not have to do it yourself. We help you choose and register your domain (for example, yourbusiness.com) in your business's name, and we take care of getting the website published.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every website launches ready for Google and we submit it on launch day. To compete for the top spots in searches like yours, our SEO and Google Maps service does the ongoing work.

Will it look good on a phone?

Yes. We design it for phones first, because that is where most of your customers see you, and then adjust it for tablets and computers.

Can I change things later?

Yes. We show you how to make the most common changes, and if you would rather we handle it, our website maintenance service covers the changes you need.

Do you only work with businesses in Medellín?

No. Our studio is in Medellín, Colombia, and we work by video call and WhatsApp with businesses across Colombia and in other countries, wherever you are.
